Raising the Standard of Civic Leadership.

The Foundation for Political Excellence develops informed, engaged, and ethical civic leaders through education, leadership development, public service, and civic technology.

Excellence in Public Service Starts With Education.

FPE believes that strong communities depend on people who understand their government, participate in civic life, and are prepared to lead with integrity.

Politics Is More Than Elections.

  • Understanding complex government systems and policies
  • Serving communities through effective local action
  • Holding public institutions accountable with integrity
